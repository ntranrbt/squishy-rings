Squishy Rings is a calming fidget toy you can hold in one hand.

Tilt your phone and watch colorful rings drift and float in a glass chamber filled with soft, slow-moving fluid. Every ring tumbles, spins, and bobs naturally, just like the real toy.

Press the big squishy button to send the rings swirling. A satisfying pop and a gentle vibration make every press feel good, while the rings slow to a lazy drift again.

There is no score, no timer, and nothing to beat. Just tilt, squish, and relax.

Designed for kids and grown-ups who love a good fidget.
